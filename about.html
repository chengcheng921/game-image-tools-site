Our Story

GameImage.tools was born from a simple frustration: every time we wanted to share a game screenshot on Discord, a forum, or Reddit, something got in the way. The file was too large. The format wasn't supported. Or we had to upload our screenshots to some random website and hope they didn't keep them.

So we built the toolkit we always wanted: fast, free, and completely private.

What Makes Us Different

  • Local-first processing. Every tool runs entirely in your browser using JavaScript and Canvas API. Your files are processed on your own device and never uploaded to a server. Once you close the tab, the data is gone.
  • No registration. No accounts, no email addresses, no passwords. Open a tool, use it, done.
  • Free forever. Our tools are supported by unobtrusive advertising — no subscriptions, no watermarks, no hidden paywalls.
  • Built for gamers. Preset aspect ratios for avatars and wallpapers, compression presets tuned for Discord's 8 MB limit, palette extraction for stream overlays and thumbnails.

What You'll Find Here

  • Image tools: compress, resize, crop, convert formats, rotate & flip, and extract color palettes.
  • Document tools: EPUB conversion, PDF merge, split, and PDF to image.
  • Developer tools: JSON formatter, Base64, URL encoding, timestamps, color conversion, and UUID generation.
  • General utilities: QR code generator, HEIC to JPG, word counter, and password generator.
