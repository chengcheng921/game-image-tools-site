How to Rotate or Flip an Image

  1. Upload — Drop a PNG, JPG, or WebP image (up to 20MB) onto the drop zone or click to browse.
  2. Rotate or flip — Use the five buttons: Rotate Left 90°, Rotate Right 90°, Rotate 180°, Flip Horizontal, or Flip Vertical. The preview updates instantly.
  3. Combine freely — Actions stack, so two left rotations equal a 180° turn, and a flip plus rotation gives you mirrored orientations.
  4. Download — Choose PNG, JPEG, or WebP and save the result.

Fix Sideways Screenshots and Upside-Down Captures

It happens to everyone: a phone capture lands sideways, a screen recording export comes out upside down, or a console screenshot saved with the wrong orientation. Instead of opening a full editor, drop the image here and tap a button. Because rotation is a geometric transform — not a filter — a 90° or 180° turn keeps every pixel perfectly crisp, so your fixed image looks identical to the original, just the right way up.

Mirror Flips for Thumbnails and Stream Overlays

Flipping horizontally is a quiet design trick: mirror a character render so it faces toward your thumbnail text, balance a stream overlay layout, or create a symmetrical background from a single artwork. A vertical flip turns a landscape into a ready-made reflection effect.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is rotating lossless?

The rotation itself loses nothing. The image is re-encoded when you download — pick PNG output for a completely lossless file, or JPEG/WebP for a smaller one.

Can I combine rotations and flips?

Yes — every click applies on top of the current state. For example, Flip Horizontal followed by Rotate 180° is the same as Flip Vertical.

What formats and sizes are supported?

PNG, JPG, and WebP images up to 20MB. Everything runs locally in your browser — no upload, no account, completely free.
