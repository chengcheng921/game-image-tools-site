How to Convert HEIC to JPG

  1. Upload HEIC files — Drag one or more .heic or .heif files onto the drop zone, or click to browse.
  2. Choose output format — JPG is great for universal compatibility. PNG preserves transparency. WebP is the smallest.
  3. Convert & download — Each file converts in a couple of seconds. Download individually or click Download All to get every image at once.

Why Are iPhone Photos HEIC Anyway?

Apple introduced HEIC in iOS 11 as a smaller, higher-quality replacement for JPEG. It looks identical to JPEG but takes about half the file size. However, many websites, Windows PCs, and older phones can't open HEIC files, which is why you need to convert before sharing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this work offline?

Yes! The conversion runs entirely in your browser. Once the page is loaded, you can disconnect from the internet.

Is there a limit on file size or number of files?

Each file can be up to 20MB. You can convert as many files as you like — there are no limits.

Will the conversion reduce photo quality?

HEIC to JPG conversion re-encodes the image. Using a quality setting of 95% (our default) produces a visually indistinguishable result from the original.
