FAQ Before You Contact

Are images uploaded to your server? No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your files never leave your device.

Is GameImage.tools free forever? Yes. Every tool on the site is free to use with no limits, registration, or signup required.

Why does my PDF show an error? Some PDFs use newer internal structures or have permission protection that browser-based tools can't parse. Try opening the PDF in your reader and saving a fresh copy, then upload again.
