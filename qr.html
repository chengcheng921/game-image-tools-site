What Is Error Correction, and Which Level Should You Pick?

QR codes embed redundant data so they still scan when partially damaged or obscured. Level L recovers 7% of the code, M handles 15%, Q 25%, and H 30%. Pick H if you plan to overlay a logo or print small; M (the default) is fine for screen use. Higher levels make denser codes, so keep the content short.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do these QR codes expire?

Never. The content is encoded directly into the pattern — there's no redirect service in the middle, so the code works as long as the link it points to is alive.

Why won't my QR code scan?

Contrast is everything: keep the foreground much darker than the background, avoid inverted colors, and don't shrink the code below about 2cm when printing.

How much text can a QR code hold?

Hundreds of characters, but capacity shrinks as error correction rises and dense codes get harder to scan. Short URLs always work best.
