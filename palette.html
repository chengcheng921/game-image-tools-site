How to Use the Color Palette Extractor

  1. Upload — Drop a screenshot or artwork onto the drop zone. PNG, JPG, and WebP up to 20MB are supported.
  2. See the auto palette — The tool instantly extracts the 12 most dominant colors and shows each as a swatch with HEX and RGB values.
  3. Pick exact pixels — Hover over the image and a magnifier lens previews the color under your cursor. Click to lock it into your Picked Colors list.
  4. Copy values — Click any swatch to copy its HEX code to the clipboard. Remove picked colors with the × button.

Auto Palette vs Manual Pixel Picking

The automatic palette answers "what colors define this image overall?" by counting quantized pixels — perfect for themes and mood boards. Manual picking answers "what is this exact shade?" — perfect when you need the precise green of a health bar or the gold of a legendary item border.

Frequently Asked Questions

What format do the copied colors use?

Clicking a swatch copies the HEX code (like #3A7BD5). The RGB values are displayed underneath every swatch if you need those instead.

How many colors can I extract?

The auto palette shows the top 12 dominant colors. On top of that you can manually pick as many individual pixels as you like — duplicates are filtered automatically.

Does it work with transparent PNGs?

Yes — fully and mostly transparent pixels are skipped so they don't pollute your palette. And as always, everything runs locally: your image is never uploaded.
