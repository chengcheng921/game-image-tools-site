How to Merge PDF Files for Free (3 Methods)

Published on Nov 5, 2026 · 4 min read

Need to combine multiple PDFs into one document? Maybe you have character sheets for your TTRPG group, a split strategy guide, or several invoices. Here are three ways to merge PDFs — from the easiest to the most powerful.

Method 1: Online PDF Merger (Easiest)

1. Go to the free PDF merge tool.

2. Drag and drop your PDF files (or click to select multiple).

3. Use the ↑ and ↓ buttons to reorder them.

4. Click "Merge and Download."

Pros: No install, no signup, works on any device. Your files never leave your browser.

Method 2: Preview on Mac

1. Open the first PDF in Preview.

2. Enable Thumbnails view (View → Thumbnails).

3. Drag additional PDFs into the sidebar.

4. Reorder pages by dragging.

5. File → Export as PDF.

Pros: Built-in, no internet needed. Cons: Mac only, tedious for many files.

Method 3: Adobe Acrobat (Pro Only)

Adobe Acrobat Pro has a "Combine Files" tool, but it costs $12.99/month. For most gamers, the free online tool is more than enough.

Join PDF Files or Merge PDF Files — Same Job

Search results use both words, but "join PDF files", "combine PDFs" and "merge PDF files" all describe one operation: appending the pages of several documents into a single file, in the order you choose. Every method below works the same whichever term you use — only the label on the button changes. The checks afterwards are also identical: confirm page order, confirm that bookmarks and form fields survived, and confirm the result still opens in a reader other than the one that produced it.

Merging PDFs Without Adobe

Acrobat is the paid route, and it is not required. There are three Adobe-free paths, and which one you pick depends on whether the files can leave your machine:

RouteCostFiles leave your device?Best for
Browser-based mergerFreeNo — runs locallySensitive documents, one-off jobs, any OS
macOS PreviewFreeNoQuick merges on a Mac, drag-to-reorder
Command line (pdfunite, qpdf)FreeNoDozens of files, repeatable scripts

On macOS or Linux, pdfunite report-1.pdf report-2.pdf combined.pdf joins files in the order you list them, and qpdf --empty --pages a.pdf b.pdf -- out.pdf does the same with more control over page ranges. Both are offline, scriptable and free. What you give up compared with Acrobat is bookmark editing and form-field handling, which rarely matters for simple joins.

Why Merge PDFs?

  • TTRPG groups: Combine character sheets, rule references, and maps into one session document.
  • Strategy guides: Merge multi-part guides into a single portable file.
  • Sharing: One file is easier to share and archive than 10.

Tip: Split After Merging

If you merge the wrong files, don't worry — use the free PDF split tool to separate pages by range. Both tools are free and run in your browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a file size limit?

Since processing happens in your browser, the limit is your device memory. Individual PDFs of 100 MB each are fine for most laptops. Very large collections can be merged in batches.

Will the merged PDF lose quality?

No. Merging only concatenates the page stream — it does not re-rasterize or re-compress content. Text stays selectable, images keep their original resolution.

Can I reorder pages while merging?

Some tools let you drag pages into order. If not, merge in the order you want and use a PDF splitter afterwards to trim any extras.

How do I join PDF files for free?

Use the browser-based merger: drop the files in, arrange the order, and download the combined PDF. It runs locally with no upload, no account and no page limit beyond your device's memory, which is also why joining is free.
