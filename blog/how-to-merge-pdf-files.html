How to Merge PDF Files for Free (3 Methods)

Published on Nov 5, 2026 · 4 min read

Need to combine multiple PDFs into one document? Maybe you have character sheets for your TTRPG group, a split strategy guide, or several invoices. Here are three ways to merge PDFs — from the easiest to the most powerful.

Method 1: Online PDF Merger (Easiest)

1. Go to the free PDF merge tool.

2. Drag and drop your PDF files (or click to select multiple).

3. Use the ↑ and ↓ buttons to reorder them.

4. Click "Merge and Download."

Pros: No install, no signup, works on any device. Your files never leave your browser.

Method 2: Preview on Mac

1. Open the first PDF in Preview.

2. Enable Thumbnails view (View → Thumbnails).

3. Drag additional PDFs into the sidebar.

4. Reorder pages by dragging.

5. File → Export as PDF.

Pros: Built-in, no internet needed. Cons: Mac only, tedious for many files.

Method 3: Adobe Acrobat (Pro Only)

Adobe Acrobat Pro has a "Combine Files" tool, but it costs $12.99/month. For most gamers, the free online tool is more than enough.

Why Merge PDFs?

  • TTRPG groups: Combine character sheets, rule references, and maps into one session document.
  • Strategy guides: Merge multi-part guides into a single portable file.
  • Sharing: One file is easier to share and archive than 10.

Tip: Split After Merging

If you merge the wrong files, don't worry — use the free PDF split tool to separate pages by range. Both tools are free and run in your browser.
