How to Crop a Screenshot on Windows and Mac

Published on Nov 10, 2026 · 5 min read

The built-in ways to capture your screen on Windows and Mac take the whole screen or a rough selection — but real screenshots usually need cleanup afterward. The taskbar sneaks in, the party chat covers the corner, or the moment you want to share is only the center third of the frame. Cropping is the fix, and you don't need Photoshop to do it well.

The Built-In Options (and Their Limits)

Windows: Snipping Tool (Win+Shift+S) lets you select a region at capture time. That works when you know exactly what you want before you shoot — but for timed gameplay moments you usually capture everything and trim later.

Mac: Cmd+Shift+4 gives area selection, and the preview thumbnail allows a quick crop. Again, it's capture-time only.

Both fall short when you need a precise aspect ratio (a 1:1 avatar, a 16:9 thumbnail) or want to review the result before saving. That's where a dedicated cropper earns its keep.

Cropping Online Without Installing Anything

Open the free online image cropper — it works in any browser on Windows, Mac, or even a Chromebook, and your screenshot never leaves your device. Everything runs locally.

Step 1: Upload the Screenshot

Drag the file onto the drop zone. PNG, JPG, and WebP up to 20MB are supported.

Step 2: Draw the Crop Box

Click and drag directly on the image to mark the area you want to keep. Then refine it:

  • Drag the round handles on the edges and corners to resize the box.
  • Drag inside the box to move the whole selection.

Step 3: Pick an Aspect Ratio (Optional but Recommended)

Matching the ratio to your destination saves a second resize later:

  • 1:1 — avatars and profile pictures
  • 16:9 — YouTube thumbnails, Steam capsules
  • 9:16 — TikTok, Shorts, Reels
  • 4:3 or 3:2 — blog headers and prints
  • Free — when you just want to cut the clutter

With a ratio locked, the box keeps its shape as you resize it, so you can't accidentally skew the frame.

Step 4: Crop, Review, Download

Click Crop and the tool shows the exact result with its pixel dimensions and file size. This confirmation step is the killer feature — if the framing feels off, hit Back to Edit and adjust. When it's right, download as PNG (lossless), JPEG, or WebP.

Pro Tips for Cleaner Crops

  • Cut toward the subject. Removing 30% of the frame around your character makes the moment feel twice as dramatic.
  • Watch the edges of UI. Half a health bar looks sloppy; crop either fully inside or fully outside HUD elements.
  • Crop first, then compress. A tighter frame means fewer pixels, which means a smaller file — run it through the compressor if you're still over a platform limit.
