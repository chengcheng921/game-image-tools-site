Free QR Code Generator: Create Custom QR Codes Online

Published on Nov 5, 2026 · 3 min read

QR codes are everywhere — on posters, merch, business cards, and game server flyers. Here's how to create custom QR codes for free, with your own colors and sizes.

5 Ways Gamers Use QR Codes

  • Game server invites: Put a QR code on your server poster — scan to join.
  • Discord invites: Share your community link on physical flyers at LAN parties.
  • Stream links: Print QR codes on merch linking to your Twitch/YouTube.
  • Tournament signups: QR code on the venue wall links to the signup form.
  • Contact info: Share your socials with one scan at gaming events.

How to Create a QR Code

1. Go to the free QR code generator.

2. Enter your URL or text (e.g., your Discord invite link).

3. Customize size (200-512 px), foreground color, and background color.

4. Choose error correction level (more on this below).

5. Click Generate, then Download PNG.

What Is Error Correction?

QR codes can be partially damaged and still scan. There are 4 levels:

  • L (7%): Recovers 7% of data. Best for clean digital use.
  • M (15%): Default. Good for most cases.
  • Q (25%): Better for printed materials.
  • H (30%): Best for merch, stickers, and anything that might get scratched.

For printed merch or stickers, always use Level H so the code still scans even if partially covered.

Custom Color Tips

  • Use dark foreground on light background (high contrast = easier scanning).
  • Avoid light-on-light or red-on-green combinations.
  • Match your brand or game theme colors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the generated QR code free forever?

Yes. Personal and commercial use is allowed. The codes do not expire, and there is no tracking embedded in them.

What size should I print a QR code?

QR codes need quiet space around them (the white border) and a minimum of about 2 cm (0.8 in) on paper for reliable scanning. Larger is always better.

Can the QR code be colored or custom-styled?

Functional QR codes should stay black on white. Colored codes, custom logos, and artistic styling can work but reduce scanning reliability — test with multiple phones before printing.
