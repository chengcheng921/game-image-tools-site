Why Turn PDF Pages Into Images?

Plenty of places accept images but not PDFs: Discord and forum posts, slide decks, social media carousels, marketplace listings, and thumbnail generators. Converting pages to PNG or JPG also makes them easy to annotate in any image editor, drop into a video timeline, or use as a background. Students convert lecture slides to images for note apps; gamers convert rulebook tables for quick reference on a phone. Everything renders locally with PDF.js, so confidential documents — invoices, contracts, medical forms — never touch a server.

PNG or JPG for Your Pages?

Pages full of text and line art look best as PNG — sharp edges, no compression smearing, at the cost of bigger files. Photo-heavy pages are fine as JPG, which is exported at 95% quality for a much smaller download. When in doubt, convert one page as both and compare.

Frequently Asked Questions

What resolution do the images have?

Pages are rendered at 2× their original size, which keeps small text sharp on high-DPI screens and when zooming.

Does it work with scanned PDFs?

Yes — every page is rendered exactly as it appears, whether the PDF contains real text or scanned images.

Why did I get a ZIP file?

Browsers block one click from downloading many files, so Download All packages every page image into a single ZIP. It's all generated locally — no upload, no account.
