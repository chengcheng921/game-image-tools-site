Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

At GameImage.tools ("we", "us", or "our"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Your Files Never Leave Your Device

The core promise of GameImage.tools is local-first processing. All image and document tools on this website (compression, resizing, cropping, format conversion, PDF tools, EPUB conversion, and more) run entirely in your web browser using client-side JavaScript.

  • Your images, PDFs, and other files are never uploaded to our servers.
  • We cannot see, access, or store any file you process with our tools.
  • When you close the page, all data is discarded automatically.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require registration and do not collect personal information such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

However, like most websites, we may collect limited, non-identifying information through third-party services:

  • Analytics data: anonymous usage statistics (pages visited, approximate location, browser type, device type) collected via Google Analytics or similar services.
  • Cookies: small text files stored by your browser. See Section 3 for details.

3. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies (such as localStorage) for the following purposes:

  • Preferences: remembering your language choice (this is stored only in your own browser and is never sent to us).
  • Analytics: understanding how visitors use the site so we can improve it.
  • Advertising: third-party advertising partners may use cookies to serve ads. See Section 4.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. All tools will continue to work without cookies.

4. Third-Party Advertising

We may display advertisements served by third-party networks, including Google AdSense. These networks may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or web beacons in their advertisements and links that appear on GameImage.tools. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs.
  • GameImage.tools has no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers.

You can consult the respective third-party privacy policies for more detailed information on their practices and instructions on how to opt out.

5. Analytics Services

We may use Google Analytics or similar analytics services to understand site usage. These services collect standard log data such as IP address (anonymized where possible), browser type, referring pages, and visit duration. This data is used in aggregate form only and does not identify you personally.

6. GDPR — Your Rights (EU/EEA Users)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Access: request information about personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Erasure: request deletion of your personal data.
  • Objection / Restriction: object to or restrict processing of your personal data.
  • Portability: receive your personal data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent: withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

Because our tools process everything locally, we generally hold no personal files or content from you. To exercise any of these rights regarding analytics or advertising data, please contact us using the information in Section 9.

7. CCPA — Your Rights (California Users)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected about them.
  • Request deletion of their personal information.
  • Opt out of the "sale" of personal information. We do not sell personal information as defined by the CCPA.
  • Not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising these rights.

8. Children's Privacy

GameImage.tools is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@gameimageeditor.com

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
